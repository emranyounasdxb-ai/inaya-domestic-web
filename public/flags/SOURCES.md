# Country flag assets

The twelve SVG country flags in this folder were downloaded unchanged from
https://flagcdn.com/{ISO-country-code}.svg on 28 October 2026. These are the same
countries already represented by the website's country guides.

Flagpedia documents programmatic downloads at https://flagpedia.net/download/api
and states that its flag images are public domain at https://flagpedia.net/terms.
Only the flag artwork is reused; no other Flagpedia content is reproduced.

Codes: lk, np, mm, ph, in, bd, et, ke, gh, ug, sl, id.
