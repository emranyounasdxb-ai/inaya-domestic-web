## Genuine review request workflow

1. The service team identifies a completed, genuine customer interaction in its own records. Do not use website form entries as proof of a completed service; the website forms currently validate locally and do not submit.
2. Use a neutral rule, such as inviting every eligible customer after service completion, irrespective of praise or complaint. Respect any communication opt-out. Do not screen by expected star rating, offer a reward, ask staff/family to post as customers, or ask a customer to change/remove a negative review. [Google's review guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122?hl=en) requires genuine experience and prohibits incentives.
3. The profile owner copies the real review link from Google Business Profile and verifies it points to INAYA's existing place. [Google's link instructions](https://support.google.com/business/answer/16816815?hl=en) describe that flow. Insert that checked link into the templates below; do not guess a URL.
4. An authorized service representative sends at most one initial request through a channel the customer agreed to use. Keep an internal log of the interaction ID, request date, language, channel, opt-out status, and link version. Do not store review text or customer details in this repository.
5. Accept all genuine outcomes. If a customer reports a problem, route it to service follow-up without withholding the review option. Replies must avoid private customer/worker details. Measure published reviews separately from requests and never report requests as new reviews.

## Case-study consent gate

Case studies need a real completed case, a fact check against service records, and explicit permission **before** publication. Ask separately for each identifiable customer's name/quote, home or location detail, photo/video, and any identifiable worker's own information or likeness. Default to anonymized, non-identifying facts; do not publish documents, contact numbers, medical/immigration details, family information, or worker profiles. Record the exact approved draft, channels, duration and consent evidence outside this public repository. If consent is withdrawn, stop future use and assess existing placements with the owner. Consent does not prove performance claims or authorize a new customer contact campaign.

**English consent draft**

> INAYA proposes to share the attached, fact-checked story about your completed service. The exact text/media to publish is: {{approved_draft}}. Proposed channels: {{channels}}. Identifying details, if any: {{details}}. Please choose whether you consent to this specific use; you may decline without affecting your service. We will not publish until we have your written approval. Contact {{approved_contact}} if you later want us to stop future use. Any identifiable worker will be asked separately.
