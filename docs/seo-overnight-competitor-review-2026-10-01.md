# INAYA competitor and buyer-question review — 1 October 2026

This is a public-source planning record, not a claim about search rank, traffic, licensing beyond the cited ministry listing, or INAYA worker availability. Competitor wording, prices, guarantees and service promises must not be reused as INAYA claims. The private capture and query context are kept outside the tracked repository.

## Selection method

Fifteen candidates were screened for household domestic-worker service overlap, audience, direct-service model, Ajman/UAE relevance, English and Arabic reach, buyer intent, official identity evidence, and amount of inspectable evidence. Each criterion was scored 0 (absent or unverified), 1 (partial), or 2 (strong) for the four closest candidates. The numbers compare this observed sample; they are not business-quality or search-ranking scores.

The [MOHRE domestic-worker service-centre list](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/assets/4d5f436c/domestic-workers-services-centers-30-05en.aspx) lists Al Saada in Ajman and Family Care in Dubai. Its listed phone numbers match the respective websites' public main contact numbers. FamilyCare's site states UAE service and has a dedicated Ajman page; the ministry list verifies its Dubai centre, not an Ajman branch. Al Saada's site lists an Ajman office. Neither competitor's self-published testimonials, review totals, guarantees, prices, training, or processing times have been independently adopted as facts about INAYA.

Other screened candidates: [Al Nuamiya](https://tadbeer-ajman.ae/) and [Marhaba](https://tadbeermarhaba.ae/) are relevant Ajman centres, but the sampled buyer queries and inspected pages gave less comparable evidence than the selected pair. Search-result directories, publishers, classifieds, clinical-care providers and cleaning-only providers were excluded from direct-competitor selection.

## Limits and next checks

The sampled Google results were personalized in a UAE/Deira browser context, so this review does not assign universal ranks. INAYA's current sitemap and on-page technical checks are strong; no new city or nationality page is justified. The website and Google Business Profile use different WhatsApp numbers; the owner confirmed that both are active and controlled, so no phone change is proposed. Next content changes should remain in the existing bilingual commercial pages, retain approved facts and layout, and pass the normal CI and FTP production gates.
